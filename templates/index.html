bearcove labs

Applied machine learning & linguistics.

bearcove labs builds a custom speech stack — speech recognition, text-to-speech, and the linguistic tooling around them — toward a best-in-class dictation app for Mac and iOS.

The work runs from on-device inference (Core ML, Metal, the Apple Neural Engine) through training and quantization, alongside research collaborations.

Content creation

bearcove publishes long-form Rust writing at fasterthanli.me and a steady stream of tech videos on YouTube.
